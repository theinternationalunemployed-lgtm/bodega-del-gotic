A proper neighbourhood bodega

An old-school bodega a stone's throw from the Correos building and Port Vell. Come for homemade tapas — patatas bravas, Galician-style octopus, pa amb tomàquet — served quickly and warmly, at fair prices for the area.

And the beer: Estrella Galicia and 1906 straight from the tank, delivered directly from the brewery and poured really fresh.
